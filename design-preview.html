01

Brand & design direction

Depth is tonal, not optical. Surfaces step up from the page floor in three restrained moves and are separated by a single hairline border. Terracotta marks the primary action and the checked or on state of a control — nothing else.

Surface hierarchy

Three levels. No shadows, no glow, no glass.

07

Accessibility

Measured results against WCAG 2.2 after the approved revision round, with the remaining exceptions stated. Nothing here is asserted without a computed ratio.

Pass

1.4.3 Text contrast

Primary foreground reaches 17.69:1 on the page floor and 13.33:1 on the lightest surface. Muted foreground stays at or above 5.75:1 everywhere it may appear. White on terracotta is 4.84:1; white on destructive is 5.10:1. Terracotta is never used as a text color.

Pass

1.4.11 Non-text contrast

Boundaries that identify a control use border-strong #6B747C: 4.09:1 on the page floor, 3.78:1 on card-bg and 3.48:1 on elevated — input, textarea, select, unchecked checkbox and the off-state switch track. The checked box and switch-on track (tertiary) reach 3.72:1 on card-bg; the invalid border (destructive) reaches 3.25:1 on elevated. The focus ring is ≥ 13.33:1 on every surface.

Pass

1.4.13 Content on hover or focus

The tooltip appears on hover and keyboard focus, can be dismissed with Escape without moving the pointer or focus, stays dismissed while that hover or focus remains, and the pointer can move onto it without it closing. The popover closes with Escape and returns focus to its trigger.

Pass

2.5.8 Target size

Every pointer target is at least 24×24 CSS px. The checkbox input is 24×24, the switch is 40×24, and the label rows that wrap them are at least 32px tall.

Pass

Keyboard & semantics

A skip-to-content link, semantic landmarks (nav, main, section with labelled headings), role="switch" with aria-checked kept in step, tab arrow-key navigation, native dialog focus trapping with Escape to close, and prefers-reduced-motion honoured for the only animation in the system.

Pass

Status never color-only

Every badge and alert carries a text label and its own icon shape — filled square, triangle, hollow square, crossed circle — so Active, Degraded, Stopped and Failed remain distinguishable without color perception.

Exceptions

Remaining exceptions

border #2A2F34 measures 1.44:1 on the page floor and 1.33:1 on card-bg. It is used only for structural and decorative lines — cards, dividers, table rows, alerts, badges — where text or a label already identifies the element, so SC 1.4.11 does not require 3:1. Disabled controls are exempt and measure 2.26:1. Secondary, outline and ghost buttons are identified by their text label rather than their boundary.
